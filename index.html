Who We Are

About CDreams Studio

Founded by a passionate team of developers, audio engineers, and creative directors, CDreams Studio was built to re-imagine music memorabilia for the modern era. We combine tactile collectible aesthetics with high-speed NFC microchips.

Specs & Features

Product Specifications

  • Dimensions: 45mm diameter Mini-CD acrylic disc.
  • NFC Technology: High-frequency NTAG213 microchip (Passive, battery-free).
  • Finish Options: Ultra-Clear Glossy or Anti-Glare Satin Matte.
  • Compatibility: Instant tap reading for iOS & Android devices.
SDG 12 Responsible Consumption & Production

SDG Advocacy Commitment

At CDreams, we actively combat electronics e-waste and paper waste by producing durable, rewritable NFC keychains using eco-conscious polymer acrylics. Instead of disposable tickets, business cards, or temporary flyers, our products offer infinitely programmable digital touchpoints.

Advocacy Message: "Keep the feeling, reduce the footprint — one tap at a time."
